The `platform/contracts` module defines the shared language of the Hive2 platform. It establishes the wire format and domain-specific schemas that allow disparate engines (Scene, Sentinel, Harbor, Hub) to communicate over the NATS JetStream bus.

## Purpose and Design Principles

The contracts act as a **Published Language** between bounded contexts. They are governed by two strict rules:

1. **Immutability**: Once an event type is published to the bus, it is considered a permanent API. Incompatible changes require a new type or a version increment, never a mutation of existing fields [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/EventEnvelope.kt8-9](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/EventEnvelope.kt#L8-L9)
2. **Versioning**: All subjects follow a `v1` naming convention (e.g., `perception.observation.v1.<bed>`) to allow for side-by-side evolution of schemas [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/perception/Observation.kt9-10](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/perception/Observation.kt#L9-L10)

## EventEnvelope (Wire Format)

Every message on the bus is wrapped in an `EventEnvelope`. This provides a uniform header for routing, auditing, and idempotency.

## Domain Contracts

### 1. Observation (Perception)

Produced by the edge (`ia-cell`), these represent raw sensor data translated into domain vocabulary (e.g., `IN_BED`, `STAFF_ENTERED`) [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/perception/Observation.kt13-22](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/perception/Observation.kt#L13-L22) It includes a `confidence` score between 0.0 and 1.0 [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/perception/Observation.kt21](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/perception/Observation.kt#L21-L21)

### 2. SceneFact & PersonState (Scene)

The `SceneEngine` consumes noisy `Observation` events and emits `SceneFact` events.

- **PersonState**: A sealed interface representing the resident's FSM, including states like `Lying`, `AttemptingExit`, and `InBathroom` [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/scene/PersonState.kt30-51](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/scene/PersonState.kt#L30-L51)
- **SceneFact**: Includes `TransitionDetected`, `DwellExceeded` (e.g., resident out of bed too long), and `StaffPresenceDetected` [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/scene/SceneFact.kt16-104](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/scene/SceneFact.kt#L16-L104)

### 3. EffectiveRules (Policy)

Produced by the `Politica Engine`, this object represents the resolved policy for a specific resident. It acts as an Anti-Corruption Layer (ACL) between policy management and the Sentinel [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/policy/EffectiveRules.kt16-18](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/policy/EffectiveRules.kt#L16-L18)

- **AlertRule**: Defines a `trigger` (e.g., `ATTEMPTING_EXIT`), `severity` (`INFO`, `WARNING`, `CRITICAL`), and `closureCondition` [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/policy/EffectiveRules.kt32-43](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/policy/EffectiveRules.kt#L32-L43)

### 4. SentinelSignal (Sentinel)

The `Sentinel` judges `SceneFact` events against `EffectiveRules`.

- **EpisodeOpened**: Marks the start of a potential incident [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/sentinel/SentinelSignal.kt35-47](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/sentinel/SentinelSignal.kt#L35-L47)
- **SuppressedWithRecord**: Proves why an alarm was _not_ raised (e.g., `STAFF_PRESENT` or `FATIGUE_BUDGET` exceeded) [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/sentinel/SentinelSignal.kt98-106](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/sentinel/SentinelSignal.kt#L98-L106)

### 5. AlarmEvent (Alarm)

Owned by the `Harbor` (harbor), this tracks the alert lifecycle from `AlertRaised` to `Delivered`, `Seen`, and `ResolvedByPresence` [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/alarm/AlarmEvent.kt17-65](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/alarm/AlarmEvent.kt#L17-L65)

### 6. CensusSnapshot (Census)

A read-only "photo" of bed assignments (`BedId` to `ResidentId`) produced by the Hub and consumed by engines to bind observations to specific people [platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/census/CensusSnapshot.kt12-21](https://github.com/kerrvisiona-sudo/hive2/blob/f8142c8f/platform/contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/manahive/contracts/census/CensusSnapshot.kt#L12-L21)

## State and Risk Categorization

The `PersonState` contract includes logic to categorize clinical states into risk groups, which simplifies downstream logic in the Sentinel.

|PersonState|StateKind|RiskGroup|
|---|---|---|
|`Lying`|`LYING`|`SAFE`|
|`SittingInBed`|`SITTING_IN_BED`|`AT_RISK`|
|`AttemptingExit`|`ATTEMPTING_EXIT`|`AT_RISK`|
|`InBathroom`|`IN_BATHROOM`|`AT_RISK`|
|`Unknown`|`UNKNOWN`|`UNKNOWN`|
