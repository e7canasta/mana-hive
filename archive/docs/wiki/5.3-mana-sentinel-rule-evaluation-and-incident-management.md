The `mana-sentinel` binary serves as the reactive intelligence layer of the system. It consumes high-level `SceneEvent` data produced by the `mana-engine`, evaluates them against clinical alarm policies, manages the lifecycle of active incidents, and coordinates the evidence collection process via `ClipWindow` management.

## SentinelEngine: The Reactive Super Loop

The `SentinelEngine` is the core orchestrator of the sentinel worker. It maintains an in-memory `IncidentManager` and `RuleEngine` to process events flowing through NATS JetStream.

### Implementation and Data Flow

The engine operates a `tokio::select!` loop [bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs68-81](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs#L68-L81) subscribing to two primary streams:

1. **Scene Events (`evt_scene`)**: High-level FSM transitions and dwell completions from the DigitalTwin [bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs56-59](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs#L56-L59)
2. **Policy Events (`evt_policy`)**: Signals to reload the `AlarmCatalog` when clinical rules are updated [bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs60-62](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs#L60-L62)

## Rule Evaluation and Categorization

The `RuleEngine` (and the `PresetEvaluator` used in testing) maps `SceneEvent` data into three primary categories defined in `EventCategory` [crates/mana-sentinel/src/evaluator.rs](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/src/evaluator.rs):

|Category|Description|Implementation Logic|
|---|---|---|
|**Off**|Normal activity; no action.|`in_bed` perception events [crates/mana-sentinel/tests/sentinel_test.rs66-81](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/tests/sentinel_test.rs#L66-L81)|
|**Notify**|Informational; logged but no alert.|`out_of_bed` perception without transition [crates/mana-sentinel/tests/sentinel_test.rs86-101](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/tests/sentinel_test.rs#L86-L101)|
|**Alarm**|Critical; triggers incident/alert.|Transitions (Lying -> Standing) or Dwell completions [crates/mana-sentinel/tests/sentinel_test.rs106-142](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/tests/sentinel_test.rs#L106-L142)|

The evaluation uses the `AlarmCatalog` [bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs9](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs#L9-L9) to determine if a specific state duration or transition violates the resident's effective policy.

## Incident Management Lifecycle

The `IncidentManager` [crates/mana-sentinel/src/incident.rs98-103](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/src/incident.rs#L98-L103) tracks the state of active alarms at the bed level. It ensures that multiple related events (e.g., a "Bed Exit" followed by a "Dwell") are grouped into a single actionable incident.

### Incident States

Incidents follow a state machine defined by `IncidentStatus` [crates/mana-sentinel/src/incident.rs10-21](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/src/incident.rs#L10-L21):

1. **CollectingEvidence**: The initial state where the `ClipWindow` is gathering scene data.
2. **WaitingForStaff**: The system has alerted and is waiting for a physical presence in the room.
3. **StaffOnSite**: Detected via `accompanied_by` field in `SceneEvent`.
4. **ClosingIncident / Closed**: Triggered when the staff departs the room [crates/mana-sentinel/src/incident.rs73-80](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/src/incident.rs#L73-L80)

### Staff Tracking

The sentinel tracks staff arrival and departure by inspecting the `accompanied_by` metadata in incoming `SceneEvent` objects [bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs145-158](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs#L145-L158)

- **Arrival**: When `accompanied_by` contains a `staff_id` and the incident is in `WaitingForStaff` [bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs161-174](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs#L161-L174)
- **Departure**: When `accompanied_by` becomes `None` after a staff member was present [bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs175-187](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs#L175-L187)

## ClipWindow Management

A `ClipWindow` represents a temporal slice of evidence surrounding an incident. The `ClipWindowManager` [crates/mana-sentinel/src/clip_window.rs34-36](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/src/clip_window.rs#L34-L36) manages these windows in memory before persisting them to `ctx-evidence` in the Hub.

- **Opening**: Triggered by an `Alarm` category event [crates/mana-sentinel/src/clip_window.rs46-63](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/src/clip_window.rs#L46-L63)
- **Collection**: Subsequent `SceneEvent` data for that bed is added to the window's `events` vector [crates/mana-sentinel/src/clip_window.rs66-81](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/src/clip_window.rs#L66-L81)
- **Closing**: Windows are closed based on `CloseCondition` (Timeout, Staff Departure, or Manual) [crates/mana-sentinel/src/clip_window.rs84-91](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-sentinel/src/clip_window.rs#L84-L91)

## Notification Pipeline (`publish_notif`)

When an event is categorized as `Notify` or `Alarm`, the sentinel publishes a `NotificationEvent` to the `evt_notif` NATS stream [bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs215-235](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/bins/mana-sentinel/src/main.rs#L215-L235)

This pipeline is consumed by `mana-vigilancia`, which is responsible for the final delivery to external clients (mobile apps, dashboards) and creating the `Alert` record in the database.
