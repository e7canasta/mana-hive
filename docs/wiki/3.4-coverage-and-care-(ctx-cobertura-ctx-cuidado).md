This section documents the bounded contexts responsible for staff organization, work shift management, and the operational execution of resident care through rounds and notes.

## 1. Coverage Context (`ctx-cobertura`)

The Coverage context manages who is authorized to work in a facility and how they are organized into groups to cover specific wings during defined work shifts. It follows a temporal validity pattern for memberships and assignments to maintain an audit trail of who was responsible for a wing at any given historical instant.

### 1.1 Staff Groups and Membership

Staff are organized into `StaffGroup` aggregates [docs/contextos/ctx-cobertura.md21](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/contextos/ctx-cobertura.md?plain=1#L21-L21) A group belongs to a single facility [crates/ctx-cobertura/src/grupos/mod.rs85-94](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cobertura/src/grupos/mod.rs#L85-L94)

Membership (`StaffGroupMembership`) is modeled as a temporal association [docs/contextos/ctx-cobertura.md26-28](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/contextos/ctx-cobertura.md?plain=1#L26-L28) Instead of simple deletion, memberships are closed by setting a `valid_to` timestamp, allowing the system to resolve group composition for historical alerts [crates/ctx-cobertura/src/grupos/sqlite.rs163-176](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cobertura/src/grupos/sqlite.rs#L163-L176)

### 1.2 Shift Grids

Each facility defines a `ShiftGrid` consisting of `FacilityShift` entities [docs/contextos/ctx-cobertura.md22](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/contextos/ctx-cobertura.md?plain=1#L22-L22)

- **Start Minute**: Shifts are defined by a `start_minute` (0-1439), representing the offset from midnight in local facility time [docs/contextos/ctx-cobertura.md63](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/contextos/ctx-cobertura.md?plain=1#L63-L63)
- **Uniqueness**: The system enforces that no two shifts in the same facility start at the same minute [docs/funcional/modelo-dominio/ctx-cobertura.md66](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/funcional/modelo-dominio/ctx-cobertura.md?plain=1#L66-L66)
- **Resolution**: The "current" shift is resolved by finding the shift with the largest `start_minute` that is less than or equal to the current local minute [crates/ctx-cobertura/src/turnos/mod.rs50-65](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cobertura/src/turnos/mod.rs#L50-L65)

### 1.3 Wing Coverage

`WingCoverage` assigns a `StaffGroup` to a specific `shift_key` within a `Wing` [docs/contextos/ctx-cobertura.md23-24](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/contextos/ctx-cobertura.md?plain=1#L23-L24)

- **Temporal Invariant**: A wing has at most one open coverage (where `valid_to` is NULL) per shift key at any time [docs/funcional/modelo-dominio/ctx-cobertura.md68](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/funcional/modelo-dominio/ctx-cobertura.md?plain=1#L68-L68)
- **Atomic Replacement**: When a new coverage is assigned via `assign_coverage_in_transaction`, the previous open coverage for that shift is automatically closed [crates/ctx-cobertura/src/cobertura/sqlite.rs49-67](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cobertura/src/cobertura/sqlite.rs#L49-L67)

## 2. Care Context (`ctx-cuidado`)

The Care context manages the operational execution of care tasks through rounds and the recording of clinical continuity via care notes.

### 2.1 Operational Rounds (`Round`)

A `Round` represents a physical visit to a wing by staff [docs/contextos/ctx-cuidado.md20-22](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/contextos/ctx-cuidado.md?plain=1#L20-L22)

- **Snapshot Pattern**: When a round starts, it takes a "photograph" of the residents and beds currently assigned to the wing [docs/contextos/ctx-cuidado.md15-16](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/contextos/ctx-cuidado.md?plain=1#L15-L16) These are stored as `RoundTask` entries. Subsequent changes to bed assignments in `ctx-poblacion` do not affect an in-progress round [docs/contextos/ctx-cuidado.md62-63](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/contextos/ctx-cuidado.md?plain=1#L62-L63)
- **Concurrency Invariant**: There is a maximum of one `in_progress` round per wing [crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/sqlite.rs108-118](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/sqlite.rs#L108-L118)
- **Completion Invariant**: A round cannot be marked as `completed` if there are any `pending` tasks [crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/sqlite.rs170-173](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/sqlite.rs#L170-L173)

### 2.2 Round Tasks (`RoundTask`)

Each task within a round tracks the status of a specific resident check.

- **State Machine**: Tasks transition from `pending` to `completed` [crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/mod.rs59-62](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/mod.rs#L59-L62)
- **Audit**: Completing a task captures the `Actor` (staff member) and the `Instante` (timestamp) [crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/mod.rs171-185](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/mod.rs#L171-L185) Reopening a task clears these fields [crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/mod.rs187-194](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/mod.rs#L187-L194)

### 2.3 Care Notes (`CareNote`)

Care notes provide a record of continuity for residents.

- **Append-Only**: Notes are treated as immutable; corrections are handled by issuing new notes [docs/contextos/ctx-cuidado.md26-28](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/contextos/ctx-cuidado.md?plain=1#L26-L28)
- **Structure**: Includes the author, resident ID, body text, and an optional duration in minutes [docs/contextos/ctx-cuidado.md65-76](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/contextos/ctx-cuidado.md?plain=1#L65-L76)

## 3. Implementation Details

### 3.1 Persistence and Schemas

Both contexts use Diesel ORM with SQLite.

- **ctx-cobertura Schema**: Includes `staff_groups`, `staff_group_members`, `facility_shifts`, and `unit_shift_coverages` [docs/funcional/modelo-dominio/ctx-cobertura.md75-113](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/funcional/modelo-dominio/ctx-cobertura.md?plain=1#L75-L113)
- **ctx-cuidado Schema**: Includes `rounds`, `round_tasks`, and `care_notes` [docs/funcional/modelo-dominio/ctx-cuidado.md70-101](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/docs/funcional/modelo-dominio/ctx-cuidado.md?plain=1#L70-L101)

### 3.2 Key Functions and Traits

|Context|Function/Trait|Purpose|
|---|---|---|
|Cobertura|`replace_grid_in_transaction`|Replaces the shift grid and closes coverages for removed shift keys [crates/ctx-cobertura/src/turnos/sqlite.rs72-140](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cobertura/src/turnos/sqlite.rs#L72-L140)|
|Cobertura|`assign_coverage_in_transaction`|Atomically closes old coverage and opens new one for a wing/shift [crates/ctx-cobertura/src/cobertura/sqlite.rs40-110](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cobertura/src/cobertura/sqlite.rs#L40-L110)|
|Cuidado|`create_round_in_transaction`|Validates that the wing is not empty and has no active rounds before starting [crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/sqlite.rs96-162](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/sqlite.rs#L96-L162)|
|Cuidado|`update_task_in_transaction`|Updates task status and enforces that tasks cannot be modified if the parent round is closed [crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/sqlite.rs253-294](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/ctx-cuidado/src/rondas/sqlite.rs#L253-L294)|

### 3.3 Integration via `mana-app`

Since bounded contexts cannot depend on each other, `mana-app` coordinates operations that cross boundaries:

- **Identity Resolution**: `mana-app` fetches user display names from `ctx-identidad` to enrich the `MemberView` returned by the coverage API [crates/mana-app/src/cobertura.rs209-221](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-app/src/cobertura.rs#L209-L221)
- **Resident Snapshots**: When starting a round, `mana-app` queries `ctx-poblacion` to get the current resident-to-bed assignments to pass as snapshots to `ctx-cuidado` [crates/mana-app/src/cuidado.rs64-94](https://github.com/pbaalerta-wq/hubp/blob/cc2edd14/crates/mana-app/src/cuidado.rs#L64-L94)
